Registered Rampage checkout slots lease a whole checkout to one thread. When your checkout is a registered slot its lease is acquired for you on dispatch: keep it across idle turns, failures and Editor restarts, and release it only at handoff, after every Editor claim is released and Unity plus its MPPM clients are closed with dirty scenes preserved. Create warm slots once from a clean, stopped registered master on the same copy-on-write filesystem, and do not touch the master while it seeds. A quarantined slot needs explicit recovery with observed completion evidence. Without registered slots, game_studio_manage claims and reservations alone coordinate checkouts and worktrees.
