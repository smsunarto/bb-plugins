List the commits in a Whiteboard session's comparison, from base to head, each with `commit`, `parentCommit`, `subject`, `author`, `authoredAt`, `fileCount`, `additions` and `deletions`. Use it to walk a multi-commit change one commit at a time: pass a `commit` to `whiteboard_session_diff`, `whiteboard_session_file`, `whiteboard_session_tree` or `whiteboard_session_source`. `version` lists a saved version's comparison.
