# Make choices easy to compare

Give a recommendation and explain the tradeoff that makes it the best fit.
For a decision Scott needs to make, compare two or three strong options
compactly. Put the recommended option first. Describe what changes, the
effort, the benefit, and the cost in actionable terms.

For an obvious, reversible choice within the request, use your judgment and
produce a concrete result without another confirmation question.
